# EP-07 study art generation instructions

These summarize the five bitmap generation briefs used with the built-in image generation tool. Outputs were copied byte-for-byte to `art/`; source hashes are in `art-provenance.json`. The exact native cells were derived separately with nearest-neighbour sampling; see `art-derivations.json`.

1. **Environment:** Reinterpret the approved EP-06 Studio room and original Coffee Break North Star as a richly furnished, pixel-art, top-down office: warm timber main floor, magenta sofa/lounge upper left, exactly two central workstations, coffee counter lower left, decorative Meeting area upper right, decorative Focus area lower right, central bottom entrance, plants/books/windows/soft lighting. Empty room, no characters, UI, words, statuses or invented product functions.
2. **20×24 candidate:** Transparent three-agent by three-pose sprite study. Ari short dark hair/sage clothing, Mina asymmetric brown hair/terracotta, Sol blond hair/navy. Columns standing, working/seated, walking; deliberately restrained low-resolution silhouettes with consistent identities; no labels, backgrounds or unsupported role props.
3. **24×32 candidate:** Independent transparent three-agent by three-pose sprite study with the same identities. More room for hair, eyes, cuffs and bent seated posture; standing, working/seated and short walk. This is a separate art option, not a scale-up of the 20×24 candidate.
4. **Portraits:** Transparent bust portraits of Ari, Mina and Sol using the same facial and clothing cues as the 24×32 candidate; straight-on, no text, badges, invented personality or role accessories.
5. **Sol coffee:** Transparent Sol in three closely related coffee poses: held mug, short walk with mug, static mug. Keep blond/navy identity; illustration only for the exact trusted coffee-break condition, not generic Waiting.

The generated artwork is a compositional exploration. Automated quantization into exact cells exposes size tradeoffs; it does not prove final pixel quality, anchor alignment or runtime occlusion.
